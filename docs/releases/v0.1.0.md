The first release: a grand piano built from the physics, as VST3 and CLAP for
Linux and Windows, x86-64 with AVX2/FMA (`x86-64-v3`).

- Modal strings under a computed hammer/felt contact, unison coupling through
  the bridge, the soundboard in the loop, sympathetic resonance, sustain pedal.
- A curated master chain each factory preset carries: shelf, bus compression,
  room, ceiling. Every setting inside it is editable on the FX page.
- The editor: the microphones are dragged on the soundboard, the strings light
  as they sound, the dampers lift with the pedal.
- Ten factory presets, and patches saved to disk as JSON.

Known and staying for now: the treble's upper partials sit above a recorded
Steinway's; a single held note is nearly mono; no macOS build; the Windows
binaries are unsigned. `LIMITATIONS.md` has the measurements.
